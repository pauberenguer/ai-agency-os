# GBP Optimization Quick Reference

## Category Selection Strategy

1. **Primary category**: Most specific category matching core service
2. **Secondary categories**: Up to 9 additional relevant categories
3. Check competitors' categories for ideas
4. Google updates category list regularly — check for new options

## Business Description Formula (750 chars max)

```
[Primary Service] in [Location]. [Business Name] provides [services list]
for [target audience]. [Unique value proposition]. [Key differentiators:
years experience, certifications, awards]. [Service area coverage].
[Call to action].
```

## Photo Strategy (Target: 50+ photos)

| Type | Count | Tips |
|------|-------|------|
| Exterior | 5+ | Different angles, daylight, signage visible |
| Interior | 5+ | Clean, well-lit, show workspace |
| Team | 5+ | Professional headshots + candid work photos |
| Work/Portfolio | 20+ | Before/after, completed projects |
| Products | 10+ | If applicable |
| Logo | 1 | High-res, square format |
| Cover | 1 | Best representing the business |

## GBP Post Types & Schedule

| Day | Post Type | Content |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| Mon | Update | Industry tip or news |
| Wed | Offer | Special deal or promotion |
| Fri | What's New | Recent project, new service, team update |

## Review Response Templates

**5-Star:** "Thank you, [Name]! We're glad you had a great experience with [specific service mentioned]. We look forward to serving you again."

**4-Star:** "Thanks for the feedback, [Name]! We're happy you enjoyed [positive aspect]. We'd love to hear how we could earn that 5th star."

**Negative:** "Thank you for sharing your experience, [Name]. We take feedback seriously and would like to make this right. Please contact us at [phone/email] so we can address your concerns directly."

## NAP Format Standard

Establish ONE format and use everywhere:
```
Business Name Inc.          (not "Business Name" or "Business Name, Inc")
123 Main Street, Suite 100  (not "123 Main St Ste 100" or "123 Main Street #100")
Dallas, TX 75201            (not "Dallas, Texas 75201")
(214) 555-0100              (not "214-555-0100" or "2145550100")
```
