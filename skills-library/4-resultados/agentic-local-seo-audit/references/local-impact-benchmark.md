# LOCAL-IMPACT Scoring Framework

**Local Optimization, Consistency, Authority & Listing — Impact Performance Assessment & Competitive Tracking**

Version: 1.0 | 60 Items | Scoring: 0-3 per item | Max Raw: 180 | Normalized: 0-100

---

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 0 | Absent | Not implemented at all |
| 1 | Weak | Partially implemented with significant gaps |
| 2 | Good | Implemented correctly with minor improvements needed |
| 3 | Excellent | Fully optimized, best-in-class implementation |

## Veto Checks

These critical items trigger automatic "CRITICAL" status regardless of overall score:

| Veto | Condition | Impact |
|------|-----------|--------|
| V01 | GBP not claimed (L01 = 0) | Cannot compete in local pack |
| V02 | Website not indexed | Zero organic visibility |
| V03 | Active Google penalty | All rankings suppressed |
| V04 | NAP completely wrong on GBP | Customers can't find/contact |
| V05 | Zero reviews | Major trust deficit |
| V06 | SSL not installed | Security warning deters visitors |

If ANY veto triggers, overall status = **CRITICAL** with a mandatory fix-first recommendation.

---

## Score Calculation

1. Sum all 60 item scores (max 180)
2. Normalize: `(sum / 180) × 100` = LOCAL-IMPACT Score
3. Check veto conditions — override to CRITICAL if any triggered

### Score Interpretation

| Range | Grade | Status |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 80-100 | A | Excellent — industry leader |
| 60-79 | B | Good — competitive with optimization opportunities |
| 40-59 | C | Fair — significant gaps to address |
| 20-39 | D | Poor — major issues affecting visibility |
| 0-19 | F | Critical — fundamental problems |
