# Keyword Classification Reference

## Intent Categories

| Intent | Signals | Content Type | Example |
|--------|---------|-------------|---------|
| Informational | how, what, why, guide, tips | Blog, guide, FAQ | "how to unclog a drain" |
| Commercial Investigation | best, top, review, vs, compare | Comparison, listicle | "best plumber in dallas" |
| Transactional | buy, hire, cost, price, book, near me | Service page, landing page | "emergency plumber near me" |
| Navigational | [brand name], [brand] login | Homepage, branded page | "joe's plumbing dallas" |
| Local | [service] + [location], near me | Location page | "plumber dallas tx" |

## Keyword Tail Classification

| Type | Words | Volume | Competition | Conversion |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Head | 1-2 | Very High | Very High | Low |
| Mid-tail | 2-3 | Moderate | Moderate | Moderate |
| Long-tail | 4+ | Low | Low | High |
| Question | 5+ | Low-Med | Low | Moderate |

## Priority Scoring Formula

```
Priority Score = Search Volume (1-5) × Commercial Intent (1-5) × Feasibility (1-5)

Volume:     1=<100, 2=100-500, 3=500-2K, 4=2K-10K, 5=10K+
Intent:     1=info only, 2=early research, 3=comparing, 4=ready to buy, 5=urgent need
Feasibility: 1=impossible, 2=very hard, 3=achievable 6-12mo, 4=achievable 3-6mo, 5=quick win

Max Score: 125
Priority: 80+ = Tier 1, 40-79 = Tier 2, <40 = Tier 3
```
