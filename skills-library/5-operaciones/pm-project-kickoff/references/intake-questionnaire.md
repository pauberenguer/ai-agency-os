# Client Intake Questionnaire

Send this at the start of every new project. Answers feed directly
into the scope_doc and project setup.

Adapt questions to your service type. Remove what doesn't apply.

---

## Section 1 — Your Business

1. What does your business do, and who are your ideal customers?
2. How would you describe your business in one sentence?
3. What sets you apart from competitors?
4. Who are your main competitors? (3–5 examples with URLs)

## Section 2 — The Project

5. What is the main goal of this project?
   (e.g., "Get more leads", "Launch online sales", "Replace outdated site")
6. What does success look like for you in 90 days after launch?
7. Is there anything about your current [website / brand / system] you
   absolutely want to keep? Anything you definitely want to change?
8. Are there any sites, designs, or examples you love? (Please share links)

## Section 3 — Your Audience

9. Who is your primary audience? (Age, job title, location, needs)
10. What action do you most want visitors to take?
    (Call, fill out a form, buy, book, sign up, etc.)
11. What objections or concerns do visitors typically have?

## Section 4 — Content

12. Do you have existing written content, or will you be creating new content?
13. Do you have photos/images, or will you need stock photography?
14. Do you have a logo? In what formats? (SVG, PNG, AI, PDF?)
15. Are there brand guidelines (colors, fonts, tone of voice)?

## Section 5 — Technical

16. Do you have an existing domain? Who is the registrar?
17. Do you have existing hosting? Provider and login?
18. Are there any third-party tools that must integrate with this project?
    (CRM, email marketing, booking, payments, etc.)
19. Who will be responsible for updating the site after launch?
    (Yourself, a team member, or {{AGENCY_NAME}}?)

## Section 6 — Timeline & Budget

20. Is there a hard deadline for this project? Why?
21. Are there any upcoming events, campaigns, or dates we should plan around?
22. [If budget not already confirmed] What is your budget range for this project?

---

## Notes for the Team

- Section 6 question 22 is only needed if budget hasn't been confirmed in the contract.
- Send as a Google Form, Typeform, or Notion form — or paste into an email.
- Answers should be reviewed before the kickoff call, not during it.
- Flag any answers that create scope conflicts (e.g., client mentions a feature
  not in the contract) — treat as potential scope additions, run through
  the scope-creep skill before committing to anything.
